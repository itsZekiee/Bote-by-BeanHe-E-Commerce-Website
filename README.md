BOTE by BeanHe is an e-commerce platform dedicated to coffee enthusiasts, showcasing the art of coffee through expertly roasted blends. The site features two signature house blends: the Columbia Subpremo, made from premium Colombian beans, and the Brazil Sultan blend, which combines smooth Brazilian coffee with locally-sourced beans for a rich flavor profile. Among the featured products are the Mocha, a luxurious blend of bold espresso, decadent chocolate, and steamed milk; the Caramel/Salted, offering a delightful mix of flavors for a sweet coffee experience; and the Dark Mocha Chip, a rich and velvety treat that combines chocolate and espresso. BOTE invites customers to savor the craftsmanship of their coffee while enjoying a seamless shopping experience.
